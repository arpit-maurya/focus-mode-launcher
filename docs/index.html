Fast ADB Provisioning

For developers and enterprise deployments, grant the required listener permission via ADB:

# Grant notification listener permission
adb shell cmd notification allow_listener \
  com.example/.system.NotificationSuppressorService

# Grant usage stats permission
adb shell pm grant com.example \
  android.permission.PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS
Requires USB debugging enabled under Android Developer Options.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the launcher track or send my data anywhere?

No. The launcher operates 100% locally on your device. Notification filtering and usage metrics are stored securely in local Room SQLite storage and never leave your phone.

Will I miss important calls or emergency alerts?

No. You can add phone calls, SMS, Slack, or any critical app to the VIP Whitelist in Settings to ensure urgent communication pings bypass the suppression shield.

Can I use this on a real E-Ink phone (e.g. Boox, Hisense)?

Yes! The high-contrast black/white layouts and static typography were specifically designed to excel on monochromatic E-Ink refresh displays without ghosting.

How do I revert to my old launcher?

Simply go to Android Settings > Apps > Default Apps > Home App and choose your previous launcher whenever you wish.
